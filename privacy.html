Maxxy Pants - Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. This policy covers the iPhone game Maxxy Pants, published by Ryan Wang.

The short version

Maxxy Pants does not collect, store or share personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (checkpoint, coins, luck, the outfits you own and your settings) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes it.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The game only learns from Apple which of its own items your Apple Account has bought, so that it can unlock and restore them. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Software the game is built with

The game is built with the Unity engine and Unity's in-app purchasing package. They are configured with analytics, advertising and diagnostics reporting turned off, and the game does not ask them to collect anything.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Real-money purchases are placed behind a parental check in the game and are subject to the purchase controls on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
