Maxxy Pants - Support

Maxxy Pants is a one-thumb walking game for iPhone. Hold to stretch a leg, let go to step, and try not to land on a crack.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: wangryan0805@gmail.com

Common questions

How do I play? Hold anywhere on the screen to stretch the leg forward, and release to put the foot down. A tap is a small step, a longer hold is a longer stride. Holding far too long ends in the splits.

Where did my progress go after a fall? Nowhere. Checkpoints are permanent: your next walk starts from the last checkpoint you reached.

I bought something and it is missing. Open Shop and tap Restore (or Settings, Restore purchases) while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Purchases are one-time and are never charged twice.

How do Mystery Boxes work? They are opened with coins earned by walking, never with money. The chances for every rarity are shown by the (i) button on the Mystery Box screen.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
